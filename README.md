## Mission Overview
Since the 1960s, NASA has left hardware across the solar system—on the Moon, on Mars, and in deep space. From rovers and scientific instruments that completed their missions to probes traveling away from Earth, this hardware varies widely in both purpose and current state.

**CosmicRelics** is an interactive, illustrated digital museum and storytelling application designed to introduce school-age space enthusiasts and the public to NASA's historic hardware left behind on alien worlds.

## Featured Exhibits
1. **Apollo 11 Lunar Module "Eagle" Descent Stage (Moon • 1969):**  
   *Location: Mare Tranquillitatis (0.67° N, 23.47° E).*  
   *Science & Story: Delivered Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin to the surface. Features the Laser Ranging Retroreflector (LRRR), which scientists still ping with Earth lasers today to measure lunar orbit with millimeter precision. Left behind because lifting the heavy descent stage into orbit would have doubled fuel requirements.*
2. **Opportunity Mars Exploration Rover ("Oppy") (Mars • 2004–2018):**  
   *Location: Meridiani Planum, Mars (1.95° S, 354.47° E).*  
   *Science & Story: Designed for a 90-sol mission, Oppy explored for 14.5 years across 45.16 km (an off-world marathon record!). Discovered hematite blueberries that proved liquid water once flowed on Mars. Left dormant after a planet-encircling dust storm blotted out the sun.*
3. **Surveyor 3 Lander (Moon • 1967):**  
   *Location: Ocean of Storms (3.02° S, 23.42° W).*  
   *Science & Story: Proved the lunar surface could support a lander's weight. Visited in 1969 by Apollo 12 astronauts Pete Conrad and Alan Bean, who retrieved its TV camera for return to Earth.*
4. **InSight Mars Geophysical Lander (Mars • 2018–2022):**  
   *Location: Elysium Planitia, Mars (4.50° N, 135.62° E).*  
   *Science & Story: Detected over 1,300 marsquakes and mapped the red planet's metallic liquid core before Martian dust gradually covered its solar panels.*
5. **Apollo 17 Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV-3) (Moon • 1972):**  
   *Location: Taurus-Littrow Valley (20.19° N, 30.77° E).*  
   *Science & Story: The electric moon buggy famous for its duct-taped map fender. Left parked 140 meters away so its camera could film the final Apollo liftoff.*
6. **Voyager 1 & The Golden Record (Interstellar Space • 1977–Present):**  
   *Location: Over 24.3 Billion km from Earth.*  
   *Science & Story: The furthest human-made object in history, carrying humanity's cosmic time capsule into the stars.*

## Interactive Features
* **Dynamic Radar Planetary Locator:** Real-time HTML5 Canvas animation showing planetary latitude/longitude coordinates and relic landing locations.
* **Archival Audio Radio Simulator:** Web Audio API sound synthesizer simulating historical CAPCOM radio chirps and telemetry beeps.
* **Youth STEM "Did You Know?" Cards:** Engaging, school-age explanations of why hardware was left behind, rocket equation mass ratios, and space weathering.
* **Curator Quiz & Badge:** Interactive challenge where students test their knowledge to earn a "Certified Junior NASA Relic Curator" credential.
* **Zero Dependencies:** Pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript.
